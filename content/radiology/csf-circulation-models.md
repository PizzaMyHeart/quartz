1. Traditional model

   - CSF produced by choroid plexus
   - Flows from 4th ventricle to basal cisterns (subarachnoid space) via median and lateral apertures
   - Unidirectional, rostrocaudal
   - Resorbed by arachnoid granulations and villi into superior sagittal sinus
   
2. Updated CSF-ISF model (glymphatic system)

   -  Astrocytes in the interstitial (subpial) space have AQP-4 channels
   -  CSF is moved from perivascular spaces around arterioles into the interstitial space
   -  CSF mops up nasty waste e.g. amyloid
   -  CSF is moved from the interstitial space into perivascular spaces around venules
