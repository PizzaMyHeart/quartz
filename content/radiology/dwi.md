# ADC

- Sample at various *b* values. If water can move freely (no restriction) signal intensity should decay as *b* increases.
- ADC map = calculated ADC = representation of rate of DWI signal intensity decay
- Faster decay = higher ADC value = brighter on ADC map
- Low ADC in: reduced extracellular space, tortuous extracellular space, increased cellularity, increased fluid viscosity
