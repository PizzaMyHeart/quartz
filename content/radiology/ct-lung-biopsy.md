CT biopsy PPV 96 - 99%, NPV 51 - 84%.
Non-diagnostic biopsies: atypical cells 50% chance of malignancy, OP 20% chance of malignancy.
17 - 28% non-diagnostic.
54% of initially non-diagnostic biopsies show diagnosis at repeat biopsy.

18G introducer or lower gauge associated with 1.55 times more pneumothorax and 1.39 times more chest tube insertion.
Needle gauge more important than number of passes for diagnostic yield.

Pneumothorax rates 26 - 27%.
Chest tube insertion 5 - 14% of procedures.
Lesions < 2 cm 10 times more likely to result in pneumothorax than lesions > 4 cm.
Lesions within 2 cm of pleura but not abutting pleura 7 times more likely to result in pneumothorax than lesions abutting the pleura.
Crossing bulla, more than one pleural puncture, anterior approach associated with higher pneumothorax risk. Anterior approach OR 1.83.
Pneumothorax  > 7 days after biopsy despite percutaneous drainage = persitent air leak, treat with pleurodesis.

Haemorrhage + haemoptysis = high-grade haemorrhage
Cavitation, moderate pulmonary hypertension associated with haemoptysis.

Air embolism 0.02 - 0.21% of cases.
Left lateral decubitus, Trendelenburg position, 100% O2.
Prone position, lesion higher than level of left atrium, and more nonlesional aerated lung sampled associated with higher risk.

Death < 0.02%, from air embolism or haemorrhage.

Tumour seeding 0.012% - 0.56% of cases.
