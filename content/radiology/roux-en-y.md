# Roux-en-Y

# Anatomy

Gastric pouch
Excluded stomach

Roux/alimentary/efferent limb
Biliopancreatic/afferent limb

Gastrojejunal anastomosis, jejunal stump (one of the two afferent limbs)
Jejunojejunal side-to-side anastomosis


# Complications

## Leak

Gastrojejunal anastomosis
Gastric pouch
Jejunal stump
Jejunojejunostomy

## Stricture

Gastrojejunal anastomosis

## Marginal ulcers (gastrojejunal anastomosis)

## Jejunal ischaemia

Acute: due to mobilisation at surgery
Chronic: results in stricture, ulcers

## SBO (open)

Obstructed Roux limb
Biliopancreatic limb obstruction (closed loop)
Common channel obstruction

## Internal hernia (laparoscopic)

Transverse mesocolon (retrocolic Roux limb)
Small bowel mesentery (jejunojejunal anastomosis)
Posterior to Roux limb (Petersen defect)

Jejunojejunal anastomosis left midabdomen (normal) -> LUQ or right midabdomen

## Intussusception

Staple line at jejunojejunal anastomosis as lead point

## Gastrogastric fistula

Dehiscence of staple line between gastric pouch and excluded stomach (or fistula between surgically transected stomach)
